# C12 · Final dataset calibration of CI probability weights
Spec: FR-C6, FR-V4, I2 · Owner: Shazil · Depends: C3, C6, C8, C9, C10, C11

**Goal:** After the initial CI pipeline, screen and benchmark are complete, fit a final CI probability-table candidate from human-reviewed data and evaluate it once on a holdout untouched by fitting and the C10 benchmark. Keep policy loss costs unchanged and human-approved. This task belongs to the final FYP-2 calibration stage alongside human-led QA calibration.

**Read first:** `docs/adr/006-ci-scope-and-rerun.md`, `docs/contracts/ci_signals.md`, `docs/contracts/ci_weights_elicitation.md`, `tests/ci_corpus/README.md`, `src/core/weights.py`, `src/core/sensitivity.py`, `src/eval/stats.py`.

**Steps**
1. Add `src/eval/calibrate_ci_weights.py` to load only corpus cases with a reviewed `label` and `label_source`; derive the approved pre-rerun evidence groups using the C6 extractor and C7 tracing output.
2. Create and save a deterministic case-level training/holdout split before fitting. Record its case IDs and dataset source in `docs/contracts/ci_weights_elicitation.md`. Both training and holdout cases must be disjoint from C10 benchmark cases. Never fit, tune, or choose a method using holdout outcomes.
3. Estimate CI priors and likelihood tables from the training cases using a documented smoothing method. Write calibrated candidate values and provenance to `src/core/weights.yaml` and the elicitation sheet. Do not alter the owner-approved loss matrix or the QA domain.
4. Evaluate the frozen candidate once on held-out cases against the pre-calibration values. Report sample counts and uncertainty; do not claim generalisation beyond the data or change the candidate based on holdout results. Do not rewrite or re-score C10's benchmark. If the corpus cannot support a meaningful split or lacks reviewed examples for a cause, stop without inventing labels or weights.
5. Add tests for deterministic splitting, exclusion of unreviewed cases, normalized fitted tables, and strict separation of holdout cases from fitting.

**Check:** `.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q tests/test_calibrate_ci_weights.py`; `make weights`; `make sensitivity`; `make test-engine`.

**Done when:** final candidate CI probability tables are reproducibly fitted from reviewed training cases, evaluated once on a previously untouched holdout, and documented with provenance and limitations; costs and QA weights are unchanged, and C10 results remain the frozen provisional-weight benchmark.
**Stop and ask if:** a cause has no reviewed training examples, there are too few cases for a meaningful holdout, label evidence is ambiguous, or the only available split would reuse C10 benchmark cases or results for fitting/tuning.
