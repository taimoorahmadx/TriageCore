# C9 · Initial CI weight proposal
Spec: FR-E8, I2 · Owner: Shazil · Depends: C8

**Goal:** Add a complete CI weight domain with documented, provisional prior and likelihood proposals. Policy costs are project decisions and must be approved by the owner before the domain is enabled. C12 will calibrate probability tables on reviewed data.

**Read first:** `AGENTS.md` rule 1, `docs/contracts/ci_signals.md`, `docs/contracts/weights_elicitation.md`, `src/core/weights.yaml`, `tests/fixtures/weights_synthetic.yaml` (`ci:` section), `docs/plan.md` §4.

**Steps**
1. Add the `ci:` domain to `src/core/weights.yaml` using the C1 causes, evidence groups and C8 action contract. Do not change existing QA values.
2. Propose complete prior and likelihood values with a rationale and source for every value. Mark these as agent-proposed and provisional in comments and `docs/contracts/ci_weights_elicitation.md`; do not present them as measured or calibrated.
3. Explain candidate loss costs and their tradeoffs in the elicitation sheet. Stop for Shazil to select/approve the cost matrix; do not enable CI until that decision is recorded. Costs express policy and are not fitted by C12.
4. Enable CI only after the owner-approved cost matrix is present. Missing and invalid weights must continue to raise.

**Check:** `make weights` prints `ci: causes=[...]` (not disabled); `make test`.

**Done when:** CI weights validate, all probability values have documented provisional provenance, and the owner-approved cost table is enabled.
**Stop and ask if:** a cause, evidence state, risk tier, or policy cost is not defined by the approved contract or owner decision.
