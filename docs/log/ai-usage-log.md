# AI Usage Log

Append-only record of where AI assistance was used (FAST Idea Guide §8.4). Entries before
2026-10-25 were moved here unchanged from the old `MILESTONES.md`; the full original file is
frozen at `reference/proposal-defense/MILESTONES-v1.md`.

Some proposal-stage entries describe screens with scripted numbers (96/94/15 scores, "50 cases,
96% accuracy, 0% FPR"). Those figures were typed into the UI and never measured; see
`AGENTS.md` ("Real vs scripted"). They are kept here because this is a historical record.

## How to add an entry

One line per non-trivial change, newest at the bottom of its milestone section:

`- *YYYY-MM-DD, <member>: <task ID> · what the AI produced (files) · what the human decided or changed · how it was verified.*`
(Same format as in `AGENTS.md`; agents append at the end of the file.)
